After Merge, the Roadmap gate re-opens and admits one next feature. Nothing is chained; nothing is admitted by absence. Orange moves send work back; bold cards are judged gates — the judge runs on the strongest model and holds the lane.

Where the moves lead

every move has a known consequence
MoveWork goes toRedone

Re-crossing: when work goes back, every later gate opens again, and its brief says what changed since you approved it.

Every gate, the same five moments

1 · Workauthors run on the lane; you may steer work roles
