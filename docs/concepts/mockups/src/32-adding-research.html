I read your note, the 3 clipped pages and the index. Three takeaways — tell me if I have them right:
1. Exporter-only is the favoured option: ~12 packages, tested without a build step.
2. Every option must target 127.0.0.1 only — that matches Local by default, open by choice and the optional exporter rule in Egress policy.

1 and 2 yes. For 3, say it plainly: hand-written means we own spec changes forever. That cost is the real reason I lean away from it.
