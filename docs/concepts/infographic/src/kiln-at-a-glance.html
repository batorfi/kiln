KILNa software factory you own

Agents research, design, write, review and verify. Independent judges check the work. People decide at every gate. Durable, recorded, local by default — one feature at a time, in your workshop.

Your models, your ruleslocal by default; private or cloud only by policy
Durablea crash interrupts work, never loses it — and never approves anything
Explainableevery decision, swap and byte that left is in the record
Calm and sequentialone lane per factory, one feature at a time

≡Shape the roadmap

What KILN is not

Not an autopilot — nothing ships without a person’s approval, live or explicitly delegated.
Not a hosted service — no account, no backend; you run it.
Not a swarm — the right agent, on the right model, one step at a time.
Not a control plane — the team space coordinates; it decides no one’s gates.
Not a dashboard on an agent — process, gates and record come first.
Not a replacement for judgment — it concentrates it where it matters.
