# The engineer at the center — infographic

![The engineer at the center](kiln-engineer-at-the-center.png)

The software engineer in the middle of KILN, in three parts:

1. **Roles and relationships** — the six roles an engineer plays (Decider, Roadmap shaper, Thinker at the bench,
   Teammate, Curator, Operator), what KILN's actors do for them (director, planner, crew, judges, knowledge keeper,
   Needs you and health), and who they work with (teammates, the team space, the code host, shared hosts, newcomers).
2. **One real day** — twelve moments in Zsolt's workshop, from Needs you at 08:30 to leaving at 17:00: what he sees and
   does, what KILN does, and where the team comes in.
3. **One feature, the full agentic lifecycle** — think, shape, admit, contract, build and prove, ship, learn — and what
   each feature teaches flowing back into the next roadmap. Closed by *only you* and *never your chore*.

Illustrative scenario (Zsolt, Anna, Bence); the concept docs govern where they differ.
