# gate-card

## Purpose
A waiting decision, in the list that points to its brief — never a place to decide.

## Anatomy
gate · factory · feature · the gate's question · evidence chips · waited · Open

## Variants
judged · unjudged · policy stop · question

## States
default · hover · focus · pressed

## Accessibility
one link/button *Open gate brief*; the whole card is not a click target for assistive tech; heading level from context.

## Content
The gate's question as the headline.

## Behaviour
Opening goes to the brief; never offers a move.

## Do and don't
Do: lead with the question. Don't: show an approve button.
