# Spacing And Layout

- A 4 px base unit; the scale is 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48.
- The frame is fixed: a 200 px navigation rail and a content area starting 30 px to its right. Content is a column or a two-column grid; nothing floats over the rail.
- Breakpoints: compact 0, regular 720, wide 1200. At compact the rail becomes a top bar with *Needs you* first; the gate brief becomes a single column with the options panel after the work.
- Density: one density for 0.2.0; a compact density mode is a later MINOR change.
