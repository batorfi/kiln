# Imagery

KILN has no photography. Illustrations are diagrammatic, flat, in token colours. The brand mark (the Fork K) appears in the rail header and on first run, unaltered, in `action.primary` on light or `action.on-rail` on dark; below 24 px the small-size mark is used (`docs/brand/README.md`).
